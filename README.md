# My Favorite Movie Series: The lord of the Rings
## About
The **Lord of the Rings** trilogy, directed by *Peter Jackson*, is based on the novels by J.R.R Tolkein. It follows a hobbit named Frodo Baggins on a quest to destroy **The One Ring**.

## The Trilogy

1. Lord of the Rings: Fellowship of the Ring (2001)
2. Lord of the Rings: The two towers (2002)
3. Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (2003)

## Main Characters

- Frodo Baggins
- Aragorn
- Samwise Gamgee
- Legolas
- Gimli
- Gandalf
